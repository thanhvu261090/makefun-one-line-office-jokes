Entry for the [make.fun bounty "One-Line Office Jokes"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-markk-st-one-line-office-jokes-a51f2bcc) — one single line that makes office life funny. The house judge compares lines two at a time; the five best split a $2 reward. Free to enter.

## The entry

> "I told my boss I'd circle back on the budget in Q1, and it's now Q4 and I'm still circling."

91 characters — a single line, well inside the 1–199 character gate. It turns on a real detail of office life ("circle back") and lands the laugh in the last few words.

## How it works

`main.py` holds the entry line as a constant, checks it against the kit's gate (UTF-8 text, 1–199 characters, not only whitespace, a single line), and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` when run with `python3 main.py`. `entry.txt` holds the same line for reference.
